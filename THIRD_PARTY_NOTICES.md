The original 192 × 192 PNG is included without modification. Its 16 frames are displayed at 48 × 48 pixels in the game; CSS displays the first frame in the profile avatars. Direction columns are down, left, up, right. Animation frames progress vertically.

The office tiles and decoration are original work created for this demonstration. The MP4 videos are external demonstration resources linked in the Tiled map; they are not included in this repository.
